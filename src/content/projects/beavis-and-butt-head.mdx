I've worked on the three most recent seasons of [Mike Judge’s *Beavis and Butt-Head*](https://www.paramountplus.com/shows/beavis-and-butt-head/), writing several episodes, including “Hunting Trip,” “Hellhole,” and “Breeding Frenzy.”

It was a thrill to help send these two idiots into the woods, into trouble, and generally toward the worst possible decision available.

As of 2026, the show is also [streaming on Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/title/70142336).
